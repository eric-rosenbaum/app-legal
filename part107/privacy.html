Part 107 Prep Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Part 107 Prep helps you study for the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot knowledge test. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Your content stays on your device

Your answers, progress and exam date are stored on your device. We don't require an account and we don't sell your data.

Usage analytics

To improve the app, we collect anonymous usage data with PostHog: which screens you view, which features you use, app version, device model, operating system and approximate country. We may record anonymized screen sessions to find usability problems; photos, camera views, images and typed text are masked in these recordings. Analytics are linked to a random identifier, not to your name or email. IP addresses are not stored.

Purchases and advertising attribution

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use Superwall to show purchase options and to know whether you have an active purchase; it receives purchase status, a random identifier and device information. If you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads ad, Apple's AdServices framework tells us which campaign and keyword led to the install, without identifying you. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete all on-device data by deleting the app. To ask us to delete analytics associated with your device, or for any privacy question, email ericrosenbaum77@gmail.com.

Part 107 Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration.

Changes

We'll update this page if this policy changes.
